## Usage

Put `Spotlight.init({ id })` in the Model, route its Messages and Commands through
`Spotlight.update(model, message, context)`, and render `ui.Spotlight(h, { ...context, model, toParentMessage })`. The
`context` holds `actions` and the options that decide what choosing one does (`filter`, `limit`, `clearQueryOnClose`,
`closeOnActionTrigger`); pass the same one to both. `update` reports `ActionTriggered` with the action's `id`, along
with `SpotlightOpened`, `SpotlightClosed` and `QueryChanged`.

Ctrl + K (⌘ + K on a Mac) opens it, as do the `open()` and `toggle()` Messages. Type to filter, move with the up and
down arrows (they wrap around), and press Enter to choose; Escape or a click outside closes it.

## Actions

Each action has an `id` and a `label`, `description`, `leftSection` and `rightSection`; `dimmedSections` (true) dims
the sections, and `highlightQuery` marks the query in each label (`highlightColor` per action). The default filter
matches labels first, then descriptions and `keywords`.

## Groups, limit and scrolling

Put actions in `{ group, actions }` to show them under a label. `limit` caps how many actions show; `scrollable` with
`maxHeight` (400) scrolls the list inside a fixed height. `footer` adds content under the list.

## Shortcuts and options

`shortcut` takes one shortcut or a list (`"mod + K"` by default), or `null` for none; shortcuts are ignored while
typing in `tagsToIgnore` (inputs, text areas and selects) unless `triggerOnContentEditable`. `disabled` renders nothing.
`closeOnActionTrigger` (true), or an action's `closeSpotlightOnTrigger`, keeps it open after a choice;
`clearQueryOnClose` (true) empties the search once it closes. `filter` replaces the default filter.

## Styles API

Parts: `search`, `actionsList`, `action`, `actionBody`, `actionLabel`, `actionDescription`, `actionSection`,
`actionsGroup`, `empty` and `footer`. The modal takes Modal's options (`size` 600, `yOffset` 80, `zIndex` 9999, a
blurred overlay and the `pop` transition by default).

## Differences from Mantine

- Spotlight opens and closes through Messages handled by `update`, not a global store; each action reports its `id`
  instead of running its own `onClick`.
- The compound components (`Spotlight.Root`, `Spotlight.Search` and the rest) are not ported: `Spotlight` takes
  `searchProps`, `nothingFound` and `footer` instead.
- The list scrolls natively instead of in a ScrollArea, and `forceOpened` is not supported.
- Groups carry `role="group"` and their label as the accessible name, as well as the visible label.
