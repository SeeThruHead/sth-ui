## Setup NavigationProgress

NavigationProgress is a thin bar fixed to the top of the page, shown while something loads. Hold
`NavigationProgress.init()` in your model (`initialProgress` and `stepInterval`, 500ms, are its options), route its
messages through `NavigationProgress.update`, and render `ui.NavigationProgress(h, { model })` once, near the root of
your view. It renders in a [Portal](/miscellaneous/portal) unless `withinPortal: false`.

## Usage

Send its messages to drive it: `Started` creeps the bar forward on each step, slowing as it fills, `Stopped` pauses it,
`Set` jumps to a value, `Incremented` and `Decremented` move it one step, `Completed` fills it and fades it out, and
`Reset` hides it at 0.

## Styles API

NavigationProgress has [Progress](/feedback/progress)'s parts, `root` and `section`; the root carries `data-mounted`
while it shows.
