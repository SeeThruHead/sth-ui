## Usage

Put `Carousel.init({ id })` in the Model, route its Messages and Commands through
`Carousel.update(model, message, context)`, and render `ui.Carousel(h, { ...context, model, toParentMessage, slides })`,
where `slides` holds each slide's children. The `context` holds embla's options (`align`, `loop`, `slidesToScroll`,
`containScroll`, `dragFree`, `skipSnaps`), `initialSlide` and `duration`; pass the same one to both. `update` reports
`SlideChanged` with the new index, and `NextSlide` and `PreviousSlide` when the controls are used.

The previous and next controls, arrow keys, Home and End, the indicators (with their own arrow keys) and dragging all
move it; a live region announces "Slide 2 of 5". The `next()`, `previous()` and `scrollToSlide(index)` Messages move it
from anywhere.

## Slide size and gap

`slideSize` (`100%`) and `slideGap` (0) take a value or one per breakpoint. `includeGapInSize` (true) counts the gap as
part of each slide's size, as Mantine does; with `type="container"` the breakpoints follow the carousel's own width
instead of the window's.

## Loop, align and contain

`loop` wraps from the last slide to the first, moving slides round so there is never a gap. `align` (`center`) places
the selected slide in the view; `containScroll` (`trimSnaps`) stops at the ends without leaving empty space, and
`keepSnaps` keeps a snap for every slide. `slidesToScroll` moves several slides at a time, or as many as fit with
`"auto"`. `dragFree` lets a drag settle anywhere (`"snap"` settles on the nearest slide), and `skipSnaps` lets a strong
flick pass several slides.

## Orientation and options

`orientation="vertical"` stacks slides top to bottom and needs a `height`. `withControls` (true), `withIndicators`,
`withKeyboardEvents` (true) and `draggable` (true) turn the parts on and off; `controlSize` (26), `controlsOffset`
(`sm`), `nextControlIcon`, `previousControlIcon` and the control labels change the controls.

## Styles API

Parts: `root`, `viewport`, `container`, `slide`, `controls`, `control`, `indicators` and `indicator`, with Mantine's data
attributes (`data-orientation`, `data-include-gap-in-size`, `data-inactive`, `data-active`, `data-type`).

## Differences from Mantine

- Embla is replaced by a port of its scroll engine (snaps, containment, slide groups, loop points, drag release);
  movement animates with a CSS transition rather than embla's physics, and right-to-left direction and plugins are not
  supported.
- Slides are passed as `slides`, not `Carousel.Slide` children, so the carousel can move slides round for the loop.
- Container breakpoints are the theme's breakpoint names, not arbitrary widths.
- Arrow keys are handled on the slides and controls, not on the whole root, so the indicators' own keys don't also move
  the carousel.
