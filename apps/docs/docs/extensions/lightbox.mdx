## Usage

Put `Lightbox.init({ id, withThumbnails })` in the Model, route its Messages and Commands through
`Lightbox.update(model, message, context)`, and render `ui.Lightbox(h, { ...context, model, toParentMessage, slides })`.
The `context` holds `slideCount` and the options that change what keys and gestures do (`loop`, `withZoom`,
`withThumbnails`, `withFullscreen`, `zoomMaxScale`, `closeOnSwipeDown`, `withKeyboardEvents`, `withSlideTransition`);
pass the same one to both. `open(index)` and `close()` build the Messages that open and close it; `update` reports
`LightboxOpened`, `LightboxClosed` and `IndexChanged`.

## Slides

Each slide is an image (`src`, `alt`, `srcSet`, `sizes`, `loading`), a video (`type: "video"`, `src`, `label`, `poster`,
`autoPlay`, `tracks`) or a custom slide (`type: "custom"`, `render(active)`). Any of them can have a `caption`, a
`thumbSrc` or a `renderThumb(active)`. Videos only play while their slide is the current one.

## Keyboard and gestures

Escape closes it; the left and right arrows move between slides, or pan a zoomed image (as do up and down); F toggles
fullscreen, T the thumbnails and Z the zoom. Keys typed into a field, or pressed on a control inside a custom slide,
are left alone. Clicking an image zooms it (`withZoom`), the wheel zooms around the pointer up to `zoomMaxScale` (3),
dragging pans it, and a downward swipe closes the lightbox unless an image is zoomed.

## Toolbar

The toolbar shows the thumbnails toggle, a counter, fullscreen, download (`withDownload`, not for custom slides) and
close. `toolbarItems(defaults)` returns the items to show, so you can add, remove or reorder them. `labels` changes any
of the texts Mantine lets you change.

## Options

`withNavigation` (true) shows the previous and next buttons, `closeOnClickOutside` closes on a click beside the slide,
`returnFocus` (true) puts focus back where it was on close, and `withinPortal` (true), `zIndex` (400) and
`transitionDuration` (200) place and time it.

## Styles API

Parts: `root`, `overlay`, `content`, `toolbar`, `toolbarGroup`, `toolbarButton`, `counter`, `slides`, `slide`,
`slideImage`, `slideVideo`, `navigation`, `navigationButton`, `caption`, `thumbnails`, `thumbnail` and `thumbnailImage`.

## Differences from Mantine

- The lightbox opens through Messages handled by `update`, not a provider and store; its slides scroll on this
  library's Carousel instead of embla, so embla options and breakpoints are not taken.
- Toolbar items take a Message as `onClick`, and `toolbarItems` receives the default items rather than a payload of
  actions.
- Pinch zoom on touch screens and right-to-left arrow keys are not ported; touch zooms by tapping as a click does.
- The compound components (`Lightbox.Root`, `Lightbox.Toolbar` and the rest) are not ported.
