## Usage

Put `Notifications.init({ id })` in the Model, route its Messages and Commands through
`Notifications.update(model, message, context)`, and render `ui.Notifications(h, { ...context, model, toParentMessage })`
once. Pass the same `context` (`position`, `limit`, `autoClose`, `transitionDuration`, `pauseResetOnHover`) to both.
Send the Messages these helpers build from anywhere in the app:

- `show({ title, message, color, icon, loading, autoClose, ... })` adds a notification and reports `Opened`.
- `hideNotification(id)` closes one and reports `Closed`.
- `updateNotification({ id, ... })` changes one in place, for example to finish a loading notification.
- `clean()` closes them all; `cleanQueue()` drops the ones waiting in the queue.

## Positions

`position` sets where notifications appear by default (`bottom-right`); each notification can choose its own. There
are six positions: `top-left`, `top-right`, `top-center`, `bottom-left`, `bottom-right` and `bottom-center`.

## Limit and queue

At most `limit` notifications (5 by default) show at each position. The rest wait in a queue, highest `priority` first,
and appear as others close.

## Auto close

Notifications close after `autoClose` milliseconds (4000 by default). A notification's own `autoClose` overrides it,
and `false` on either keeps the notification open. Hovering any notification pauses every timer; set
`pauseResetOnHover="notification"` to pause only the one under the pointer. `withAutoCloseProgress` shows a bar that
fills until the notification closes.

## Swipe to dismiss

Drag a notification sideways past a third of its width, or flick it, to dismiss it; a horizontal wheel or trackpad
scroll does the same. `allowDragDismiss` and `allowScrollDismiss` turn them off; a notification with
`allowClose: false` cannot be dismissed or closed by the user.

## Stacked layout

`layout="stacked"` piles each position's notifications on top of each other, newest in front. Hovering or focusing the
stack spreads it out; on a touch screen a tap spreads it until you tap elsewhere.

## Custom notifications

`renderNotification(h, entry, close)` draws every notification itself; `icons` maps a notification's `icon` name to
what the default Notification shows.

## Styles API

Parts: `root` (each position's container), `notification` and `progress`. `containerWidth` (440), `notificationMaxHeight`
(200), `zIndex` and `transitionDuration` (250) set its sizes and timing; `withinPortal` (true) renders it in a portal.

## Differences from Mantine

- Notifications are shown by Messages handled by `update`, not by global functions and a store; ids are given or
  numbered by the model, not random. `onOpen` and `onClose` are the `Opened` and `Closed` out messages.
- A notification's `message`, `title` and `icon` are text and names, since a Model cannot hold views; `icons` and
  `renderNotification` turn them into views.
- `notifications.promise` is not ported: show a loading notification and update it from your own Command's result.
- A stacked notification counts focus and hover together when deciding whether to spread the stack.
