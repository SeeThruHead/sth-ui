## Usage

`schedule.MonthView(h, { date, events })` shows the month containing `date`. Each week lays its events out as bars in
rows, multi-day events run across the days they cover (square at an edge where they continue into another week), and
a day shows at most `maxEventsPerDay` (2) rows with a "+N more" button for the rest. Recurring events are expanded for
the weeks shown.

`onDateChange` gets the first day of the previous or next month, or today; `onDayClick` and `onWeekNumberClick` report
clicks on a day or a week number. Pass the view's `model` (from `DayView/model.ts`) with `toParentMessage` to open the
"+N more" popovers, drag events to another day at the same time, drag across days to select them, drop items from
outside and open the agenda. Arrow keys move between days and weeks.

## Days and weeks

`firstDayOfWeek` (Monday) starts each week and `withWeekendDays={false}` leaves out `weekendDays`. `withOutsideDays`
shows the days of the months around this one, and `consistentWeeks` always shows six weeks. `withWeekDays` shows the
weekday names in `weekdayFormat` (`ddd`), `withWeekNumbers` adds a column of week numbers, and `highlightToday` circles
today's date when `now` is given. `getDayProps` and `getWeekNumberProps` add attributes to a day or a week number.

## Differences from Mantine

- The header shows the month as a title (`headerFormat`, `MMMM YYYY`) rather than month and year selects.
- `now` is a prop rather than read from the clock.
- `getDayProps`, `getWeekNumberProps`, the control props and `renderEvent` work with attribute lists instead of React
  props, and the "+N more" popover is shared, opening for one day at a time.
