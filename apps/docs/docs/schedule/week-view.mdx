## Usage

`schedule.WeekView(h, { date, events })` shows the week containing `date`. Timed events sit in their day's column,
laid out side by side when they overlap; all-day and multi-day events run across the all-day row, cut off with a
square edge where they continue past the week. Recurring events are expanded for the week.

Like [DayView](/schedule/day-view), it keeps no state of its own: `onDateChange` gets the date a week before or after,
or today, and `onDayClick` the day whose heading was clicked. Pass the view's `model` (from `DayView/model.ts`) with
`toParentMessage` to drag events between days and times (all-day events move to the day they are dropped on,
keeping their length), resize them, select slots, drop items from outside and open
the agenda; `update` reports the same out messages as the day view.

## Days and labels

`firstDayOfWeek` (Monday) starts the week, and `withWeekendDays={false}` leaves out `weekendDays` (Saturday and
Sunday); weekend headings are red. `weekdayFormat` (`ddd`) formats the headings, `withWeekNumber` shows the week number
in the corner, and `highlightToday` circles today's date. The header's label is `weekLabelFormat` (`MMM DD`) for the
first and last day plus the year, or whatever `renderWeekLabel` returns.

## Time range, slots and the current time

`startTime`, `endTime`, `intervalMinutes` (60), `slotLabelInterval`, `slotLabelFormat`, `slotHeight`,
`allDaySlotHeight`, `withSubHourGridLines`, `withAllDaySlots`, `highlightBusinessHours`, `businessHours`,
`startScrollTime` and `maxHeight` work as in the day view. Arrow keys move between slots and across days. Give `now` to draw the current time line on its day;
`forceCurrentTimeIndicator` draws it on the same weekday of any week shown.

## Differences from Mantine

- `now` is a prop rather than read from the clock.
- Clicking a weekday heading calls `onDayClick` instead of both `onViewChange("day")` and `onDateChange`, since a click
  sends one Message.
- The current time line is drawn on its day's column only, with its time shown when that is the first column.
