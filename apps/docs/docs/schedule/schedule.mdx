## Usage

`schedule.Schedule(h, { model, toParentMessage, events })` shows the [DayView](/schedule/day-view),
[WeekView](/schedule/week-view), [MonthView](/schedule/month-view) or [YearView](/schedule/year-view) for the date and
view in its model, and switches between them from the header's tabs; clicking a month in the year view opens that
month. Keep `Schedule.init({ id, date, view })` (from `Schedule/model.ts`, starting on the week view) in your model
and route its Messages through `Schedule.update(model, message, context)`, mapping the Commands it returns. `update`
reports `DateChanged` and `ViewChanged` along with the views' own out messages, such as `EventDropped`.

The options shared by the views (`locale`, `radius`, `labels`, `mode`, `renderEventBody`, `renderEvent`,
`withEventsDragAndDrop`, `withEventResize`, `withDragSlotSelect`, `withExternalEventDrop`,
`withInteractiveBackgroundEvents`, `withAgenda`, the click handlers and `recurrenceExpansionLimit`) are given once;
`dayViewProps`, `weekViewProps`, `monthViewProps`, `yearViewProps` and `mobileMonthViewProps` set the rest per view.

## Layout

`layout="responsive"` shows the [MobileMonthView](/schedule/mobile-month-view) instead of the day, week and month views
when the schedule is 600px wide or less; its back button opens the year view, and the day picked on it is kept in the
model.

## Differences from Mantine

- The date, view and selected day live in the schedule's model rather than being controlled or uncontrolled props, and
  changes come back as out messages.
- Interactions are configured on the model's `update` (its `context`) rather than through callbacks on the component.
