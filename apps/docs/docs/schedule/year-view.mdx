## Usage

`schedule.YearView(h, { date, events })` shows the year containing `date` as twelve months, three to a row (two, then
one, as the view gets narrower). A day with events shows up to three dots in their colors; multi-day events mark every
day they cover, and recurring events are expanded for the year.

`onDateChange` gets the same date a year before or after, or today. `onDayClick`, `onWeekNumberClick` and
`onMonthClick` report clicks on a day, a week number or a month's name. Arrow keys move between days in reading order,
on into the next week and month.

## Months and days

`monthLabelFormat` (`MMMM`) names each month and `weekdayFormat` (`dd`) the weekdays shown with `withWeekDays`.
`firstDayOfWeek`, `weekendDays`, `withWeekendDays`, `withOutsideDays`, `withWeekNumbers` and `highlightToday` work as in
the [MonthView](/schedule/month-view). `renderDay` replaces a day's content, receiving the day and its events, and
`getDayProps` and `getWeekNumberProps` add attributes.

## Differences from Mantine

- The header shows the year as a title (`headerFormat`, `YYYY`) rather than month and year selects.
- `now` is a prop rather than read from the clock.
- `getDayProps`, `getWeekNumberProps` and the control props work with attribute lists instead of React props.
