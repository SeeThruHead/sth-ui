## Usage

`schedule.ResourcesWeekView(h, { date, resources, events })` shows the week containing `date` with a row per resource
and the days running left to right, each split into its time slots. Timed events sit in their day; all-day and
multi-day events run as bars across the days they cover, stacked when they overlap. It shares its options with the
[ResourcesDayView](/schedule/resources-day-view) (`groups`, `slotWidth`, `rowHeight`, `maxEventsPerTimeSlot`,
`renderResourceLabel` and the rest) and adds the week's: `firstDayOfWeek`, `weekendDays`, `withWeekendDays`,
`weekdayFormat` (`ddd D`) for the day headings, `highlightToday`, and `weekLabelFormat` (`MMM DD`) or
`renderWeekLabel` for the header.

With the view's `model`, bookings can be dragged to another day, time or resource, resized and selected, and the
out messages carry the resource the pointer ended on. `startScrollDateTime` scrolls to a time when the view first shows.

## Differences from Mantine

- Today is marked with a dot after its heading rather than a filled circle.
- The current time is drawn as a line in today's day of each row, without a time bubble.
- `now` is a prop rather than read from the clock, and the control props and `renderEvent` work with attribute lists.
