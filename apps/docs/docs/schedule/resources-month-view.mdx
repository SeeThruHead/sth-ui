## Usage

`schedule.ResourcesMonthView(h, { date, resources, events })` shows the month containing `date` with a row per
resource and a cell per day. Bookings run as bars across their days, stacked in rows; when a day holds more than
`maxEventsPerTimeSlot` (2) the rest go behind a "+N more" button. `groups`, `renderGroupLabel`, `renderResourceLabel`,
`dayWidth` (80px), `rowHeight` (64px), `weekdayFormat` (`ddd`), `withWeekendDays`, `weekendDays` and `highlightToday`
shape the grid, and `startScrollDate` scrolls to a day when the view first shows. `onDayClick` receives the resource
and the date of the cell clicked.

With the view's `model`, bookings can be dragged to another day or resource (keeping their time of day), stretched by
whole days from either end, and days selected by dragging across a resource's cells, which reports midnight on the
first day to `23:59:59` on the last. Arrow keys move between days and resources.

## Differences from Mantine

- The header shows the month as a title (`headerFormat`, `MMMM YYYY`) rather than month and year selects.
- `onDayClick` receives the resource with the date, since a cell belongs to one resource.
- `now` is a prop rather than read from the clock, and the control props and `renderEvent` work with attribute lists.
