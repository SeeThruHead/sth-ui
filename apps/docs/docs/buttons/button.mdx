`xs` to `xl` or `compact-xs` to `compact-xl`.

## Button group

Mantine's `Button.Group` and `Button.GroupSection` are `ui.ButtonGroup` and `ui.ButtonGroupSection`. Mantine
styles the buttons inside a group with child selectors, which StyleX does not have, so the group takes its
buttons and sections as items and tells each where it sits: `groupButton(buttonProps)` and
`groupSection(sectionProps)`. Joined edges lose their rounding and share `borderWidth` (default 1px), half on
each side.

<Example story="buttons-buttongroup--with-section" caption="Two buttons around a section" />

<Example story="buttons-buttongroup--vertical" caption="Vertical, with a 2px border" />

A section takes Button's `size`, `radius`, `color`, `variant`, `gradient` and `autoContrast`, but has no hover
state. Its part is `groupSection`; the group's is `group`.
