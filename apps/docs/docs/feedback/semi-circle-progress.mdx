## Usage

SemiCircleProgress shows a `value` from 0 to 100 on a half circle. `size` (200) and `thickness` (12) set its
dimensions, `orientation` (`up` or `down`) and `fillDirection` (`left-to-right` or `right-to-left`) which way it
fills, and `filledSegmentColor` its colour.

## Styles API

Its parts are `root`, `svg`, `emptySegment`, `filledSegment` and `label`. The label carries `data-position` and
`data-orientation`.
