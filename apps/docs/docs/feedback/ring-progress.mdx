## Usage

RingProgress draws `sections` (each a `value` in percent and a `color`) around a ring, with an optional `label`
in the middle.

<Configurator story="feedback-ringprogress--configurator" />

## Size, thickness & rounded caps

`size` (120 by default) and `thickness` (12, at most a quarter of the size) shape the ring; `roundCaps` rounds the
ends of the first and last sections.

<Example
  height={240}
  story="feedback-ringprogress--size"
  caption="Default, 180 with round caps, and a clamped thickness"
/>

## Section gaps

`sectionGap` leaves that many degrees between sections.

<Example height={520} story="feedback-ringprogress--section-gap" caption="No gap, 5° and 10°" />

## Start angle

`startAngle` (270, the top, by default) is where the first section starts.

<Example height={200} story="feedback-ringprogress--start-angle" caption="Right, bottom, left and top" />

## Background color

`rootColor` colours the empty part of the ring.

<Example height={180} story="feedback-ringprogress--root-color" caption="Red behind a yellow section" />

## Section events

Each section can send `onMouseEnter`, `onMouseLeave` and `onClick` messages, for example to show which part is
hovered.

## Customize label

<Example height={180} story="feedback-ringprogress--label" caption="A percentage, and an icon" />

## Filled segment transition

`transitionDuration` animates a section as its value changes.

## Differences from Mantine

- Section tooltips need Tooltip.Floating, which is not ported yet.
- The transition demo steps through fixed values instead of random ones, as the house rules keep randomness out of
  components and demos.
