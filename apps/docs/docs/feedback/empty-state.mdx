## Usage

EmptyState is a placeholder for "no data" situations: empty search results, empty tables and lists, first-run
states or error illustrations with a call to action. Give it an `icon`, a `title`, a `description` and `actions`:

<Configurator story="feedback-emptystate--configurator" />

## Compound parts

Mantine's `EmptyState.Indicator`, `.Title`, `.Description` and `.Actions` are the `icon`, `title`, `description`
and `actions` props here, rendered in that order; anything in `children` follows them in the body.

<Example height={320} story="feedback-emptystate--compound" caption="Two actions and a note after them" />

## Variant

`variant: "filled"` or `"light"` puts the icon in a coloured circle; `color` picks the colour (the primary colour by
default). Without a variant the icon is dimmed.

<Example height={300} story="feedback-emptystate--variant" caption="Light blue and filled teal" />

## Indicator background

`withIndicatorBackground` puts a neutral circle behind the icon without a variant:

<Example height={340} story="feedback-emptystate--indicator-background" caption="Neutral background" />

## Align and size

`align` is `center` (a centred column, the default), `left` or `right` (the icon beside the content). `size` (`xs` to
`xl`, `md` by default) sets the icon, the gaps and the title and description font sizes.

<Example height={300} story="feedback-emptystate--align" caption="Left and right" />

<Example height={260} story="feedback-emptystate--sizes" caption="xs to xl" />

## Title heading level

The title is a `div` by default. Set `titleOrder` (1 to 6) to render it as an `h1` to `h6` heading.

## Styles API

Its parts are `root`, `body`, `indicator`, `title`, `description` and `actions`. The root carries `data-align` and,
with a variant, `data-variant`; the indicator carries `data-with-background` when it has a background.

## Accessibility

- The root has no landmark role.
- The title is not a heading unless `titleOrder` is set.
- Give action buttons accessible names.

## Differences from Mantine

- The compound parts are props: `icon`, `title`, `description`, `actions` and `titleOrder` (Mantine's
  `EmptyState.Title order`).
