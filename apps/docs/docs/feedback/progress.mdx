## Usage

Progress shows how far along a task is: `value` is a percentage, `color` the bar's colour (the primary colour by
default), `size` `xs` to `xl` (or pixels) and `radius` the corners. `striped` adds stripes and `animated` moves them.

<Configurator story="feedback-progress--configurator" />

## Compound components

`ProgressRoot` takes several `sections`, each with its own `value`, `color` and an optional `label` inside it.

<Example height={80} story="feedback-progress--compound" caption="Documents, photos and other" />

## Vertical orientation

`orientation: "vertical"` fills from the bottom; give it a height. `autoContrast` picks a readable label colour for
light sections.

<Example height={260} story="feedback-progress--vertical" caption="Plain, striped and in sections" />

## Section width transition

`transitionDuration` (100ms by default) animates a section as its value changes.

## Accessibility

Each section is a `role="progressbar"` with its value; name it with `label` on Progress or `ariaLabel` on a section,
and turn the roles off with `withAria: false` when the bar is only decoration.

## Differences from Mantine

- `ProgressRoot` takes its sections as data rather than `Progress.Section` and `Progress.Label` children.
- Section tooltips (Mantine wraps sections in Tooltip) are not ported yet.
