## Usage

Pill is a small rounded label, the item MultiSelect and TagsInput show for each value. `size` runs from `xs` to
`xl` (`sm` by default), `radius` rounds it (fully by default), and `variant="contrast"` gives it a white background
for filled inputs.

<Configurator story="combobox-pill--configurator" />

## Inside inputs

`withRemoveButton` adds a close button; clicking it sends `onRemove`. `PillGroup` lays pills out in a wrapping row
spaced by its `size` (or `gap`). Inside an input, render the input as a `div` with `multiline`.

<Example height={180} story="combobox-pill--within-input" caption="Ten removable pills in an input" />

## Disabled

`disabled` greys the pill and hides its remove button.

## Accessibility

The remove button is `aria-hidden` and out of the tab order, as in Mantine: inside an input, Backspace removes the
last pill instead.

## Differences from Mantine

- PillGroup does not pass its `size` or `disabled` down to its pills; give each pill its own.
- Dragging pills to reorder them is not ported yet.
