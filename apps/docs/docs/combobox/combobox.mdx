## Usage

Combobox is an input with a list of options to pick from or search, built on `@foldkit/ui`'s combobox (which handles
the keyboard, focus, the active option and the floating list). Keep `Combobox.Model` (from
`Combobox.init({ id })`) and the chosen `value` in your model, pass its messages through `Combobox.update`, and take
the new value from its `Selected` out message. Select, Autocomplete and MultiSelect are built on it.

<Example height={320} story="inputs-combobox--usage" caption="Pick a grocery" />

## Data

`data` is a list of strings, or of `{ value, label, disabled, group }` items. `value` is the chosen item's value;
the input shows its label. To start with a value, give `init` its label as `inputValue`.

## Search

Typing filters the options (case-insensitive, on the label); `filter` replaces the rule and `limit` caps how many
are shown. When nothing matches, `nothingFoundMessage` is shown instead; without one the list hides.

<Example height={320} story="inputs-combobox--searchable" caption="Type to search 29 groceries" />

## Options groups

Items with a `group` are listed under that group's label.

<Example height={320} story="inputs-combobox--groups" caption="Fruits and vegetables" />

## Scrollable list

`maxDropdownHeight` limits the list's height; the rest scrolls.

<Example height={320} story="inputs-combobox--scroll" caption="At most 200px tall" />

## Input props

Combobox takes Input's `size`, `radius`, `variant`, `label`, `description`, `error`, `required`, `disabled` and
`readOnly`, plus `withCheckIcon` (a check next to the chosen option) and `withChevron` (the toggle button).

## Accessibility

The input is a `role="combobox"` named by its `label` (or `ariaLabel`), controlling a `role="listbox"` of
`role="option"` items with `aria-activedescendant` following the arrow keys.

## Differences from Mantine

- Mantine's Combobox is a set of parts (`Combobox.Target`, `Combobox.Dropdown`, `Combobox.Option`) driven by a
  `useCombobox` store, for building your own select. Here it is one component with the store in your model; the
  options are rendered from `data`.
- As in Mantine, opening the list (by focus, click or arrow key) makes the chosen option active, the option the keys
  reach is blue (`data-combobox-selected`), and an option under the pointer is only tinted gray, though Enter still
  picks the option under the pointer. Opening with the chevron button starts with no active option.
- `hiddenDropdown` hides the list while keeping the field's keyboard handling.
- The list fits the space left in the viewport and scrolls natively, rather than in a ScrollArea.
- `Combobox.Search`, `Combobox.Header` and `Combobox.Footer` belong to dropdowns opened from another target, which is
  [ComboboxPopover](/combobox/combobox-popover): its `searchable`, `header` and `footer`. Combobox's own field is the
  search box. Mantine has no built-in virtualization either.
