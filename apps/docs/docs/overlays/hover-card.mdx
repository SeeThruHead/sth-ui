## Usage

HoverCard opens a card when the pointer rests on its target, and keeps it open while the pointer is over the target
or the card. Keep `HoverCard.Model` (from `HoverCard.init({ id })`) in your model and pass its messages to
`HoverCard.update`. `target` renders the trigger from the attributes it is given. It takes Popover's `position`,
`offset`, `width`, `shadow`, `radius`, arrow and portal options.

## Delays

`openDelay` (0 by default) and `closeDelay` (150 by default) are set in `HoverCard.init`.

<Example height={160} story="overlays-hovercard--delay" caption="A slow open and a slow close" />

## Keyboard interactions

Focusing the target with the keyboard opens the card; Escape closes it and returns focus to the target.

## Dropdown role

`role` sets the card's role (`dialog` by default) and the target's `aria-haspopup`; use `tooltip` for a card that
only explains its target. `withRoles={false}` leaves the ARIA attributes off.

<Example height={180} story="overlays-hovercard--role" caption="A tooltip-like card" />

## Accessibility

The target gets `aria-haspopup`, `aria-expanded` and, while open, `aria-controls` naming the card.

## Differences from Mantine

- `HoverCard.Group` (shared delays) and the `events` and `interactive` props are not ported. Moving from the target to
  the card is covered by `closeDelay` rather than a safe triangle.
- `initiallyOpened` is an `init` option; `onOpen` and `onClose` are the `Opened` and `Closed` out messages.
- `data-position` on the dropdown is the requested `position`; the side it lands on after flipping is in
  `data-placement`.
