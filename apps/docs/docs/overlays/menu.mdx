## Usage

Menu is built on `@foldkit/ui`'s accessible menu, so its state lives in your model: keep `Menu.Model` (from
`Menu.init({ id })`) and pass its messages through `Menu.update`, whose `outMessage` is `Selected` with the chosen
item's `value`. `items` are Mantine's `Menu.Item`s: a `value`, a `label`, `leftSection` and `rightSection`, a
`color` and `disabled`. Items that share a `group` are shown together, with that group's entry in `labels` as its
`Menu.Label` and a `Menu.Divider` between groups.

<Example height={440} story="overlays-menu--usage" caption="Click Toggle menu and choose an item" />

## Controlled

`Menu.open(model)` and `Menu.close(model)` open and close it from your own messages, and `Menu.selectItem(model,
value, index)` chooses an item, as Mantine's `opened` and `onChange`.

## Hover trigger

`trigger="hover"` opens the menu while the pointer is over the target or the dropdown, and `"click-hover"` also opens
and closes it on click. `openDelay` (0) and `closeDelay` (100) set how long the pointer must stay or leave, in
milliseconds.

## Context menu

Give `contextMenu: { children, disabled, longPressDelay }` instead of `target` to open the menu with a right-click
anywhere on `children`, at the pointer, in place of the browser's own menu. On a touch screen, pressing and holding
for `longPressDelay` milliseconds (500 by default) opens it at the finger; lifting or moving the finger sooner
cancels it. `disabled` leaves the browser's menu alone.

<Example height={460} story="overlays-menu--context-menu" caption="Right-click either box" />

## Checkbox and radio items

Give an item `kind: "checkbox"` or `kind: "radio"` and `checked`, and it becomes a `menuitemcheckbox` or
`menuitemradio` with `aria-checked`, and a check or a dot in front of its label while checked (`checkIcon` replaces
both). Choosing one sends `Selected` as usual; your update flips `checked`. These items keep the menu open, unless
the item sets `closeMenuOnClick: true`.

`closeOnItemClick: false` keeps the menu open for plain items too. Because the menu's update decides whether to
close, pass the same `items` and `closeOnItemClick` to it: `Menu.update(model, message, { items, closeOnItemClick })`.

`alignItemsLabels` lines labels up: `with-indicators` (the default) leaves room for the indicator on checkbox and
radio items, `all` on every item, and `none` shows it only on checked items.

<Example height={300} story="overlays-menu--selectable-items" caption="Open View, Sort and Density" />

## Submenus

Give an item `kind: "submenu"` and pass `submenus: { [value]: { model, toParentMessage, items, labels, width } }`:
the item gets a chevron, `aria-haspopup="menu"` and `aria-expanded`, and its submenu opens to the right when the
pointer rests on the item, when it is clicked, or with Arrow Right or Enter while it is active. Arrow Left or Escape
closes the submenu and returns to the item. Each submenu is a menu with its own model in yours, as in Mantine's
`Menu.Sub`; pass the parent's `items` to `Menu.update` so it stays open while focus is in a submenu. Close the
parent when a submenu item is chosen, and reset the submenus when the parent closes.

<Example height={360} story="overlays-menu--submenus" caption="Open File, then Share or Export" />

## Search

`search: { value, onChange, placeholder }` puts a search field at the top of the dropdown, as Mantine's
`Menu.Search`. It takes focus when the menu opens; typing goes into the field, while Arrow Up and Down, Home, End,
Enter and Escape still move through and choose the items. Filter `items` by the value yourself. Pass
`search: true` to `Menu.update` too, so moving focus into the field doesn't close the menu; Tab still closes it.
Clear the value when the menu closes if you want Mantine's `clearSearchOnClose`.

<Example height={420} story="overlays-menu--search" caption="Open it and type" />

## Disabled items

A `disabled` item stays in the list but can't be chosen, by pointer or keyboard; arrow keys skip it. Groups without
a label still get a divider between them.

<Example height={400} story="overlays-menu--disabled-items" caption="Gallery and Search are disabled" />

## Dropdown position

`position` (default `bottom`), `offset` (default 8), `width` (a number, any CSS width, or `target` for the
target's width), `radius`, `shadow`, `zIndex` (300) and `withinPortal` (true) work as on
[Popover](/overlays/popover), whose look the dropdown shares.

<Example height={240} story="overlays-menu--position" caption="right-start, 16px away, 14rem wide" />

## Arrow

`withArrow` adds an arrow pointing at the target, styled like [Popover](/overlays/popover)'s: `arrowSize` (7),
`arrowOffset` (5, from the dropdown's edge when the position ends in `-start` or `-end`), `arrowRadius` (0) and
`arrowPosition` (`side`, or `center` for the middle of the dropdown).

<Example height={320} story="overlays-menu--arrow" caption="Open it: bottom-start, a 10px arrow 12px from the edge" />

## Custom component as target

`target` gets `renderRoot` and passes it to the component that opens the menu, Mantine's `renderRoot`: the
component hands its own attributes and content to the menu's button instead of rendering its root element, so it
looks like itself and the menu keeps the button's keyboard and ARIA behaviour. Button takes `renderRoot`; give the
target Button no `onClick`, as the menu's button handles clicks.

## Accessibility

Menu follows the WAI-ARIA menu button pattern: the dropdown has `role="menu"` and `aria-labelledby` pointing at the
target, the target has `aria-haspopup="menu"`, `aria-expanded` and, while open, `aria-controls`; each item has `role="menuitem"` (`menuitemcheckbox` or `menuitemradio`, with `aria-checked`, for checkbox and radio items), a labelled group has `role="group"` named by its label, and dividers have `role="separator"`.

- Enter, Space or Arrow Down on the target opens the menu on the first item, Arrow Up on the last.
- Arrow keys move between items, Home and End jump to the first and last, and typing a letter jumps to the next item
  whose `searchText` (default its `value`) starts with it.
- Enter or Space chooses the active item; Escape or a click outside closes the menu and returns focus to the
  target, and Tab closes it as focus moves on.

## Differences from Mantine

- State lives in your model (`Menu.Model`), and the chosen item arrives as an out message instead of each item's
  `onClick`.
- Items are data (`items`, `labels`) instead of `Menu.Item`, `Menu.Label` and `Menu.Divider` children; a divider sits
  between each pair of groups.
- `arrowPosition: "center"` centres the arrow on the dropdown, not on the target.
- `Menu.ContextMenu` is the `contextMenu` prop.
- Checkbox and radio items are items with `kind` and `checked` instead of `Menu.CheckboxItem`, `Menu.RadioItem` and
  their groups: your model holds what is checked. `closeOnItemClick` and the items are also passed to
  `Menu.update`, which decides whether the menu closes.
- `Menu.Search` is the `search` prop; your update filters the items and clears the value on close.
- `Menu.Sub` is an item with `kind: "submenu"` and an entry in `submenus`; each submenu's model lives in yours, and
  your update closes the parent when a submenu item is chosen.
