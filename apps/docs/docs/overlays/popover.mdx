## Usage

Popover is built on `@foldkit/ui`'s accessible popover, so its state lives in your model: keep `Popover.Model`
(from `Popover.init({ id })`) and pass its messages through `Popover.update`. `target` builds the element that
opens it, and gets the attributes to put on it as `bind`; every sth-ui component takes `bind`, so a Button works as
Mantine's `Popover.Target` child. `children` is the dropdown, Mantine's `Popover.Dropdown`.

<Configurator story="overlays-popover--configurator" />

## Controlled

`Popover.open(model)` and `Popover.close(model)` open and close it from your own messages, and `update` reports
`Opened` and `Closed` out messages, as Mantine's `onOpen` and `onClose`.

## Same width

`width: "target"` makes the dropdown as wide as the target, for input dropdowns:

<Example story="overlays-popover--same-width" caption="A 280px target and a 280px dropdown" />

## Position and offset

`position` is `top`, `right`, `bottom` (default) or `left`, each with `-start` and `-end`; it flips and shifts to
stay on screen. `offset` (default 8) is the gap to the target. `withinPortal: false` keeps the dropdown where it is
rendered instead of moving it to the page's portal root. `zIndex` defaults to 300.

<Example

## Dropdown arrow

`withArrow` adds the arrow. `arrowSize` (7), `arrowOffset` (5) and `arrowRadius` (0) shape it; `arrowPosition`
`side` (default) puts it `arrowOffset` from the edge for `-start` and `-end` positions, `center` centres it on the
target. The arrow follows the side the dropdown ends up on.

<Example height={200} story="overlays-popover--arrow" caption="bottom-start, arrow at the side" />

## Disabled

`disabled` keeps the dropdown closed, whatever the target does:

## Accessibility

The target gets `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`; focus moves into the dropdown when it opens and back to the
target on Escape. A click outside, or focus leaving the dropdown, closes it.

## Differences from Mantine

- State lives in your model (`Popover.Model`), as with every stateful sth-ui component.
- `closeOnEscape`, `closeOnClickOutside` and `returnFocus` are `@foldkit/ui`'s behaviour and always on; `trapFocus`,
  `withOverlay`, `middlewares` and `keepMounted` are not ported yet.
- `data-position` on the dropdown is the requested `position`; the side it lands on after flipping is in
  `data-placement`.
