## Position, size and offset

`position` is `left` (default), `right`, `top` or `bottom`, and picks the slide animation to match. `size` is
`xs` to `xl` (default `md`, 440px), pixels or any CSS length (`30%`), across the panel's short side. `offset`
keeps it away from the window's edges, and `radius` rounds it (square by default).

## State lives in your app

Exactly as for [Modal](/overlays/modal): `Drawer.init`, `Drawer.open`, and `Drawer.update` for its messages; the
out messages are `Opened`, `Closed`, `EnteredTransition` and `ExitedTransition`. Every Modal option except its
size and position ones applies (`title`, `withCloseButton`, `withOverlay`, `closeOnEscape`, `trapFocus`...).
