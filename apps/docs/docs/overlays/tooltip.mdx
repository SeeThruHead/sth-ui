## Usage

Tooltip is built on `@foldkit/ui`'s accessible tooltip, so its state lives in your model: keep `Tooltip.Model` (from
`Tooltip.init({ id })`) and pass its messages through `Tooltip.update`. `label` is what it says, and `target` builds
the element it describes.

<Configurator story="overlays-tooltip--configurator" />

## Tooltip target

Mantine's Tooltip wraps a single child and clones it with a ref. Here `target` gets the attributes to put on the
element as `bind`, and every sth-ui component takes `bind`, so any of them can be the target. The bundle makes the
target a `button` and links it to the tooltip with `aria-describedby`, so use a Button, an ActionIcon or another
button-like component.

## Color

`color` takes any theme color or CSS color and fills the tooltip with it; `autoContrast` picks a readable text
color for light fills. Without it the tooltip is dark in the light scheme and light in the dark one.

<Example height={160} story="overlays-tooltip--color" caption="blue, grape and teal tooltips" />

## Offset

`offset` (default 5) is the gap between the target and the tooltip. `position` is `top` (default), `right`,
`bottom` or `left`, each with `-start` and `-end`; the tooltip flips and shifts to stay on screen.

<Example height={160} story="overlays-tooltip--offset" caption="position right, offset 20" />

## Arrow

`withArrow` adds the arrow, a small square rotated 45 degrees in the tooltip's color. `arrowSize` (4),
`arrowOffset` (5) and `arrowRadius` (0) shape it; `arrowPosition` `side` (default) puts it `arrowOffset` from the
edge for `-start` and `-end` positions, `center` centres it on the tooltip.

<Example height={160} story="overlays-tooltip--arrow" caption="top-start with the arrow at the side" />

## Controlled

`opened` shows or hides the tooltip whatever the pointer does, as Mantine's `opened`; the examples above use it to
stay open. `disabled` keeps it closed.

## Multiline

The label stays on one line unless `multiline` lets it wrap; give the tooltip a width with `w`.

<Example height={220} story="overlays-tooltip--multiline" caption="Hover the button: a 220px wrapped label" />

## Close and open delay

`Tooltip.init({ id, openDelay, closeDelay })` sets how long the pointer must stay on the target before the tooltip
opens, and how long it stays open after the pointer leaves. Coming back before the close delay ends keeps it open.

<Example height={160} story="overlays-tooltip--delay" caption="No delay, a 500ms open delay and a 500ms close delay" />

## Tooltip.Group

`Tooltip.updateGroup(models, index, message, { openDelay, closeDelay })` updates a list of tooltips that share their
delays: the first waits for `openDelay`, and moving to another tooltip of the group while one is open shows the next at
once and closes the last.

<Example height={160} story="overlays-tooltip--group" caption="Hover Button 1, then move to Button 2" />

## Interactive, keepMounted and transition

`interactive: true` in `Tooltip.init` lets the pointer move onto the tooltip (its links and buttons work) without it
closing. `keepMounted` keeps the tooltip in the DOM, hidden, while it is closed. `transitionProps.duration` sets how
long it fades in.

<Example

## Tooltip.Floating

`ui.TooltipFloating` follows the pointer while it is over the target, placed by `position` (default `right`) at
`offset` (default 10) from it. Its state is `TooltipFloating.initFloating`, `FloatingMessage` and `updateFloating`.

## Accessibility

The tooltip has `role="tooltip"` and the target points at it with `aria-describedby`, so screen readers read the
label as the target's description. It shows on hover and on keyboard focus (not on a mouse click's focus), and
Escape hides it until the pointer or focus leaves. It never takes pointer events or focus, so put links and buttons
in a [Popover](/overlays/popover) instead.

## Differences from Mantine

- State lives in your model (`Tooltip.Model`), as with every stateful sth-ui component.
- It shows on keyboard focus as well as on hover, which Mantine leaves off by default (`events.focus`); `events`
  is not configurable.
- `inline` and `arrowPosition: "merge"` are not ported. `transitionProps` takes only a duration (it fades in).
- `Tooltip.Group` is a function over your list of tooltip models, `Tooltip.updateGroup`, rather than a wrapping
  component, and an interactive tooltip stays open for at least 100ms after the pointer leaves the target, the time
  to cross the gap to the tooltip (Mantine uses a safe polygon).
- `arrowPosition: "center"` centres the arrow on the tooltip, not on the target, so it can sit off the target's
  centre when the tooltip is shifted to stay on screen.
