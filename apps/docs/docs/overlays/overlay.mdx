## Colour, gradient and blur

<Example story="overlays-overlay--variants" caption="Default, a white tint, a gradient, and a blur" />

`color` (any CSS colour or theme colour) and `backgroundOpacity` (default 0.6) make the tint; `gradient` replaces
it with a CSS gradient. `blur` blurs what is behind it. `radius` rounds it to match a card, and `zIndex`
defaults to the theme's modal layer.

## Content

`center` centres its children, for a call to action over a card.

<Example story="overlays-overlay--centered-content" caption="A centred button over a card" />

`fixed` covers the window instead of its parent, and `onClick` takes a Message, which is how a modal closes when
you click its backdrop.
