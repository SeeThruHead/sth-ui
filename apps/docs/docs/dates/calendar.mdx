## Usage

Calendar shows a month of days. The header pages to the previous or next month, and its label goes up a level: to the
months of the year, then the years of the decade; choosing a year or a month comes back down. Calendar is a Foldkit
submodel: keep `Calendar.init({ id, date })` in the Model, route its Messages through `Calendar.update`, and render it
with `model` and `toParentMessage`. `update` returns out messages (`DateChanged`, `LevelChanged`, `MonthSelected`,
`YearSelected`) for Mantine's `onDateChange`, `onLevelChange`, `onMonthSelect` and `onYearSelect`.

Calendar does not select dates itself: give it `getDayProps` and `onDayClick` (or use
DatePicker).

## Columns, limits, levels and labels

- `numberOfColumns` shows several months (or years, or decades) side by side, and `columnsToScroll` pages by fewer.
- `minDate` and `maxDate` disable days, months and years outside them and the header controls that would leave them.
- `minLevel` and `maxLevel` bound the levels; pass the same context to `Calendar.update`.
- `monthLabelFormat`, `yearLabelFormat`, `decadeLabelFormat`, `monthsListFormat` and `yearsListFormat` take dayjs
  tokens, and `ariaLabels`, `nextLabel` and `previousLabel` name the controls.
- `static` draws a calendar that cannot be used; `fullWidth`, `size`, `headerControlsOrder`, `nextIcon` and
  `previousIcon` change its look. Everything [Month](/dates/getting-started) takes (`firstDayOfWeek`, `weekendDays`,
  `excludeDate`, `renderDay`, `withWeekNumbers`, `hideOutsideDates`, `today` and the rest) passes through.

## Keyboard

The arrow keys move between days (or months, or years) across all the columns, skipping disabled and outside ones, as
Mantine's do. With `enableKeyboardNavigation` (on by default), Ctrl or Cmd with ArrowUp and ArrowDown pages by a year,
adding Shift pages by a decade, and Y goes up to the year level.

## Differences from Mantine

- It is a submodel (`init`, `update`, Messages and out messages) rather than a controlled or uncontrolled component; `date` and `level` live in its Model.
- `today` is a prop, since a view cannot read the clock.
- `withNativeLevelSelect` is not supported.
