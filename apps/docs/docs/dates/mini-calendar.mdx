## Usage

MiniCalendar shows `numberOfDays` days (7 by default) from `date`, each with its month (`monthLabelFormat`, `"MMM"` by
default) and day number. The controls page by `numberOfDays` through `onDateChange`, picking a day calls `onChange`,
and `value` marks the selected day. Days outside `minDate` and `maxDate` are disabled, and so is a control that
would page past them. `getDayProps` overrides a day's `disabled` and `selected` state and adds attributes.

## Differences from Mantine

- `date` is required and kept by the parent: there is no uncontrolled `defaultDate`, and `onDateChange` gets the date
  to show next (Mantine's `onNext` and `onPrevious` are that message).
- A function `monthLabelFormat` is `formatMonthLabel`; `nextControlProps` and `previousControlProps` are
  `nextLabel`, `previousLabel`, `nextIcon` and `previousIcon`.
