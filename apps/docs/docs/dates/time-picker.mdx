## Usage

TimePicker is an Input holding a spin field for hours and minutes (and seconds with `withSeconds`, and AM/PM with
`format="12h"`). Typing fills a field and moves on when no other digit could follow; the arrow keys step by
`hoursStep`, `minutesStep` and `secondsStep`, Home and End jump to the limits, Backspace empties a field and then
moves back, and ArrowLeft and ArrowRight move between fields. Pasting a time fills every field. The value
(`"HH:mm"` or `"HH:mm:ss"`, always 24 hour) is reported once every shown field is filled, and as `""` once it stops
being whole, as Mantine does.

Keep `TimePicker.init({ id, value }, context)` in the Model and route its Messages through
`TimePicker.update(model, message, context)`, passing the same context (`format`, `withSeconds`, `min`, `max`, the
steps, `type`, `readOnly`, `disabled`) to the view. `update` reports `Changed`, `Focused` and `Blurred`, and returns
the Commands that move focus.

## Dropdown, presets and limits

- `withDropdown` shows columns of hours, minutes, seconds and AM/PM while a field has focus; `reverseTimeControlsList`
  and `maxDropdownContentHeight` change them.
- `presets`, a list of times or of labelled groups, replaces the columns; `closeDropdownOnPresetSelect` closes it on a
  pick.
- `min` and `max` clamp the value when focus leaves the fields, and clamp a paste.
- `type="duration"` allows any number of hours (`minHoursDigits` wide) and has no dropdown.
- `clearable` adds a clear button, and `name` writes the value into a hidden input.

## Differences from Mantine

- It is a submodel; the context goes to `init`, `update` and the view.
- Clicking the label focuses the hours field through the root, since the label points at the input box.
- A paste outside `min`/`max` shows the clamped time; Mantine shows what was pasted while reporting the clamped one.
- The dropdown columns scroll to the selected value when they open, not on every change.
