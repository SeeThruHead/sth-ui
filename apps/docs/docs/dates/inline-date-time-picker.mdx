## Usage

InlineDateTimePicker is a [DatePicker](/dates/date-picker) with a [TimePicker](/dates/time-picker) and a submit button
below it while the month is shown. Values are `"YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm:ss"` strings. Picking a day puts it at the time in
the TimePicker (or `defaultTimeValue`), clamped to `minDate` and `maxDate`, and moves focus to the hours; changing the
time changes the picked day's time. On the days of `minDate` and `maxDate` the TimePicker is limited to their times.
Enter in the time fields or the submit button reports `Submitted`.

`type: "range"` shows the range above a start and an end TimePicker. Keep
`InlineDateTimePicker.init({ id, today, type, value }, context)` in the Model and route its Messages and Commands
through `update(model, message, context)`; it reports `Changed` and `Submitted`.

## Differences from Mantine

- It is a submodel; the context (limits, `withSeconds`, `defaultTimeValue`, the TimePicker's `format`) goes to `init`,
  `update` and the view.
- `submitButtonProps` is `submitLabel`; `timePickerProps` and `endTimePickerProps` take the TimePicker's view props.
- `valueFormat` takes dayjs tokens (date tokens and `HH`, `mm`, `ss`), not a function.
