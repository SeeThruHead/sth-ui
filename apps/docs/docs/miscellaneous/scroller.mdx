## Usage

Scroller is a horizontal scroll container with chevron controls that appear when its content overflows. The content
also scrolls with a trackpad, Shift and the mouse wheel, or touch. Keep `Scroller.Model` (from
`Scroller.init({ id })`) in your model and pass its messages through `Scroller.update(model, message, context)`,
where the context carries `scrollAmount` and `draggable`.

<Example height={120} story="miscellaneous-scroller--usage" caption="Twenty badges" />

## Mouse drag scrolling

The content can be dragged with the mouse, as `draggable` is on by default; `draggable: false` turns it off. The
click that ends a drag is swallowed, so dragging across a button does not press it.

<Example height={120} story="miscellaneous-scroller--draggable" caption="Drag the badges" />

## Scroll amount

`scrollAmount` is how far the controls scroll, 200px by default.

<Example height={120} story="miscellaneous-scroller--scroll-amount" caption="300px per click" />

## Control size

`controlSize` sets the width of the controls, 50px by default, as a number of pixels or any CSS length.

<Example height={120} story="miscellaneous-scroller--control-size" caption="70px controls" />

## Custom icons

`startControlIcon` and `endControlIcon` replace the chevrons.

<Example height={120} story="miscellaneous-scroller--custom-icons" caption="Arrow icons" />

## Controls

`showStartControl` and `showEndControl` keep a control visible whatever the scroll position. `edgeGradientColor`
sets the colour the controls fade from (the body colour by default), and `startControlLabel` and `endControlLabel`
name them ("Scroll left" and "Scroll right" by default). A hidden control carries `data-hidden` and leaves the tab
order. In a right-to-left layout the controls swap sides and scroll the other way.

## Styles API

Its parts are `root`, `container`, `content`, `control` and `chevron`. Controls carry `data-position` (`start` or
`end`), and the container carries `data-draggable` and, while dragging, `data-dragging`.

## Differences from Mantine

- `startControlProps` and `endControlProps` are `startControlLabel`, `endControlLabel` and the Styles API.
- The scroll state lives in your model, so `Scroller.update` needs your context.
