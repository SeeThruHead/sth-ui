## Usage

ScrollArea scrolls its content with the browser's own scrolling and draws thin scrollbars over it. Keep
`ScrollArea.Model` (from `ScrollArea.init({ id })`) in your model, pass its messages to `ScrollArea.update` with
`{ type, scrollHideDelay }`, and lift `ScrollArea.subscriptions` so dragging a thumb follows the pointer.

<Configurator story="miscellaneous-scrollarea--configurator" />

## Scrollbar type

`type` decides when the scrollbars show:

- `hover` (default): while the pointer is over the area, and for `scrollHideDelay` ms (1000) after it leaves
- `scroll`: while scrolling, and for `scrollHideDelay` ms after
- `auto`: whenever the content overflows
- `always`: always, even when nothing overflows
- `never`: never; the area still scrolls

<Example height={420} story="miscellaneous-scrollarea--types" caption="auto, always, scroll and never" />

## Offset size

`offsetScrollbars` pads the content so the scrollbars don't cover it: `true` (or `xy`) for both, `x` or `y` for one, or
`present` for only the scrollbars showing. The padding is the scrollbar size unless `scrollAreaVars.offsetSize` is set.

## Disable horizontal scrollbars

`scrollbars` is `xy` (default), `x`, `y`, or `false` for none.

<Example height={260} story="miscellaneous-scrollarea--scrollbars" caption='scrollbars="y"' />

## Vertical scrollbar position

`verticalScrollbarPosition` puts the vertical scrollbar on the `left` or `right` whatever the text direction.

<Example height={260} story="miscellaneous-scrollarea--vertical-scrollbar-position" caption="Left and right" />

## Subscribe to scroll position changes

`update` returns a `ScrollPositionChanged` out message with `x` and `y` on every scroll.

<Example height={300} story="miscellaneous-scrollarea--scroll-position" caption="The position, as you scroll" />

## Scroll boundary callbacks

`TopReached`, `BottomReached`, `LeftReached` and `RightReached` out messages fire when a scroll reaches an edge.

<Example height={320} story="miscellaneous-scrollarea--boundaries" caption="Counting each edge reached" />

## Scroll to position

`ScrollArea.scrollTo(model, { top, left, behavior })` is a command that scrolls the area.

<Example height={300} story="miscellaneous-scrollarea--scroll-to" caption="Bottom, center and top" />

## Start scroll position

`startScrollPosition` scrolls the area once, when it first renders.

<Example height={260} story="miscellaneous-scrollarea--start-scroll-position" caption="Starts 250px down" />

## ScrollArea.Autosize

`ScrollAreaAutosize` grows with its content up to its `mah`, then scrolls. Its `OverflowChanged` out message says when
the content starts or stops overflowing (not on the first render, as in Mantine).

<Example height={460} story="miscellaneous-scrollarea--autosize" caption="Up to 300px tall" />

## Differences from Mantine

- `viewportRef` and `viewportProps` are not ported: use `ScrollArea.scrollTo` and the out messages instead.
- Scrolling an element into view (Mantine's `use-scroll-into-view` hook) is not ported yet.
- Over the scrollbars, the wheel scrolls the area as in Mantine; elsewhere the browser scrolls it natively.
