## Usage

Marquee scrolls its children in an endless loop. It repeats them `repeat` times (4 by default) and slides the row
by one copy every `duration` milliseconds (100000 by default), with `gap` between items.

<Configurator story="datadisplay-marquee--configurator" />

## Pause on hover

`pauseOnHover` stops the animation while the pointer is over it.

<Example height={120} story="datadisplay-marquee--pause-on-hover" caption="Hover to pause" />

## Vertical orientation

`orientation="vertical"` scrolls upwards; give it a height.

<Example height={360} story="datadisplay-marquee--vertical" caption="A 300px tall column" />

## Multiple rows

`reverse` runs the animation the other way, for rows that move against each other.

<Example height={160} story="datadisplay-marquee--multiple-rows" caption="Two rows in opposite directions" />

## Fade edges

The edges fade into the background by default; `fadeEdgeSize` (5%) and `fadeEdgeColor` change the fade, and
`fadeEdges: false` removes it.

<Example height={360} story="datadisplay-marquee--fade-edges" caption="Default, larger, coloured and no fade" />

## Accessibility

Only the first copy of the children is read by screen readers; the repeats are `aria-hidden`.

## Differences from Mantine

- The demos scroll library badges where Mantine's scroll its logo.
- The repeated copies are `aria-hidden`, so screen readers do not read the content four times.
