## Usage

The `drag({ axis, axisThreshold, threshold, filterTaps, tapThreshold })` Mount sends `Dragged` for each step of a
pointer drag that starts on the element: `xy`, `initial`, `movement` (from the start), `delta` (since the last event),
`distance`, `direction`, `velocity` (pixels per millisecond, 0 when released after a 100ms pause), `elapsedTime`, and
`first`, `last`, `active`, `tap` and `canceled`. Nothing is sent until the pointer moves past `threshold` (a number or
`[x, y]`); `filterTaps` reports a short press as a `tap`, within `tapThreshold` (3px). `axis` keeps only `x` or `y`, or
`lock` chooses the dominant axis once the movement passes `axisThreshold` (1px). Other buttons, other pointers and a
second press are ignored, and text selection is turned off on the page while dragging. `dragStep` is the pure step
behind it.

## Differences from Mantine

- It is a Mount sending `Dragged` rather than a hook calling a handler; `enabled` is leaving the Mount out, and the
  handler's `cancel()` is not available (a `pointercancel` still ends the drag with `canceled: true`).
- The event itself is not included, since a Message carries data.
