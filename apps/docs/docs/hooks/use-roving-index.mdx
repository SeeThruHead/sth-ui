## Usage

Put the `rovingIndex({ orientation, loop, dir, columns, activateOnFocus })` Mount on the container, mark each item
with `data-roving-index`, and give the item whose index is in the Model `tabindex="0"` and the rest `-1`. The arrow keys,
Home and End move focus as Mantine's do (wrapping unless `loop: false`, mirrored in right to left, skipping items with
`aria-disabled`; in a grid, by row and column, with Ctrl+Home and Ctrl+End for the whole grid), and the Mount sends
`RovingFocused` with the new index, also on click. `rovingMove` is the pure key handling.

## Differences from Mantine

- It is one Mount on the container rather than `getItemProps` on each item; `onFocusChange` is the `RovingFocused` Message.
