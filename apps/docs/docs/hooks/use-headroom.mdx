## Usage

`headroom({ fixedAt, scrollDistance })` is a Stream of `{ pinned, scrollProgress, events }` from the window scroll:
`scrollProgress` goes from 1 (shown) to 0 (hidden) over `scrollDistance` (100px) while scrolling down past `fixedAt`
(0), and back while scrolling up, measured from where the direction last changed, as Mantine's is; `pinned` is
`scrollProgress > 0`, and `events` carries `pin`, `release` and `fix` where Mantine calls `onPin`, `onRelease` and
`onFix`. `headroomStep` is the pure step behind it. `scrollDirection` is a Stream of `up` or `down`, ignoring scrolls
for 300ms after a resize, as Mantine's `useScrollDirection` does.

## Differences from Mantine

- They are Streams for a Subscription; the callbacks arrive as `events` with each change.
