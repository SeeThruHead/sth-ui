## Usage

`applyConstraints(sizes, panels, handle, delta, redistribute)` moves a handle within each pane's `min` and `max`,
collapsing collapsible panes dragged under their threshold and taking space from the nearest panes or all of them
(`redistribute`). `resizeFrom`, `collapse`, `expand`, `toggleCollapse`, `reset` and `keyboard` give the next sizes for a
drag, a collapse, a double click and the arrow, Home and End keys, keeping each size in the unit it was given in
(number, `%`, `px`, `rem`). [Splitter](/layout/splitter) is built on them and adds the dragging.

## Differences from Mantine

- The sizing is pure functions over the sizes in the Model; dragging is Splitter's Subscription rather than a hook's handlers.
