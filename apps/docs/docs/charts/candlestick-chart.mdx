## Usage

`data` is a list of rows with `open`, `high`, `low` and `close` prices, and `dataKey` names the x-axis field. Each row
is a candle: a wick from low to high and a body from open to close, in `upColor` (teal) when it closed at or above its
open and `downColor` (red) otherwise. The y-axis covers the lowest low to the highest high with 5% to spare. Rows
missing a price are skipped. Hovering a column shows its four prices.

## Keys, labels, colours and sizes

- `dataKeys` names the four price fields, and `labels` renames them in the tooltip.
- `upColor`, `downColor`, `maxCandleWidth` and `candleStrokeWidth` (1) style the candles.
- `valueFormatter` and `unit` format prices, `referenceLines` marks levels, and `xAxisLabel` and `yAxisLabel` name the
  axes.
- The grid, axes and tick lines take the same props as [BarChart](/charts/bar-chart).

## Differences from Mantine

- `referenceAreas`, `referenceDots`, `xAxisProps`, `yAxisProps`, `gridProps`, `tooltipProps`, `composedChartProps` and
  `children` are recharts props and are not supported.
