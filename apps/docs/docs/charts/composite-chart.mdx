## Usage

CompositeChart draws each series by its `type`: `line`, `area` (filled at 0.2 opacity) or `bar`. It takes the same
data, axes, grid, legend and tooltip props as [LineChart](/charts/line-chart). With bar series the x axis has bands
and the lines and areas pass through the middle of each band; `maxBarWidth` caps the bars' width.

## Lines and areas only

Without bar series the x axis runs edge to edge, as in LineChart.

<Example height={300} story="charts-compositechart--lines" caption="A dashed line and an area, with a legend" />

## Differences from Mantine

- It draws its own SVG and HTML rather than recharts; `lineProps`, `areaProps`, `barProps`, `composedChartProps` and
  children are not supported.
- Not ported yet: a right y axis, point labels, the brush and reference areas.
