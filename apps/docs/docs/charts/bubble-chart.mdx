## Usage

BubbleChart draws one row of bubbles: `dataKey.x` names each bubble's category on the x axis, `dataKey.y` its height
in the row, and `dataKey.z` its size. `range: [min, max]` gives the bubbles' areas in square pixels for the smallest
and largest `z`. `label` names the row on the left, and `color` colours the bubbles. Hovering a bubble shows its
category and `z`, formatted by `valueFormatter`.

## Colours and tooltip

`textColor` and `gridColor` colour the labels and the axis, and `withTooltip: false` turns the tooltip off.

## Differences from Mantine

- It lays itself out in HTML rather than recharts; `xAxisProps`, `yAxisProps`, `zAxisProps`, `scatterProps` and
  `tooltipProps` are not supported, and there is no hover cursor.
