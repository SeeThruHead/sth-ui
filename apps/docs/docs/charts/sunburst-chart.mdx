## Usage

`data` is a list of `{ name, value, color, children }` nodes. Each level of the tree is a ring, starting at
`innerRadius` (50): an arc's angle is its value against the total (a node without a value adds up its children), and
it takes its own `color` or its parent's. As in recharts, the rings split the radius by the tree's depth plus one, so
the outer edge is left clear. Hovering an arc shows its value.

## Labels, gaps and angles

- `withLabels` names each arc in its middle.
- `gap` (1) and `strokeColor` draw the lines between arcs.
- `size` (300) sizes the chart, and `startAngle` (0) and `endAngle` (360) draw part of a circle, counter-clockwise
  from three o'clock.
- `valueFormatter` formats the tooltip value; `withTooltip={false}` turns it off.

## Differences from Mantine

- `dataKey`, `sunburstChartProps`, `tooltipProps` and `children` are recharts props and are not supported.
