## Usage

`data` is a list of rows, `dataKey` the row field shown on the x axis, and `series` the fields drawn as lines, each
`{ name, color, label?, strokeDasharray? }`. Give the chart a height with `h`. Hovering the chart shows a tooltip with
every series' value at the nearest point, a dashed cursor and enlarged dots.

## Options

- `withLegend` adds a legend above the chart; hovering a series in it fades the others.
- `unit` follows each value on the y axis and in the tooltip, and `valueFormatter` formats them.
- `gridAxis` (`x`: horizontal lines, `y`, `xy` or `none`) and `tickLine` (`y`, `x`, `xy` or `none`) choose the grid
  lines and tick marks, and `strokeDasharray` the grid's dash pattern.
- `referenceLines: [{ y, label, color }]` draws horizontal lines across the chart.
- `xAxisLabel` and `yAxisLabel` name the axes.
- `textColor` and `gridColor` colour the labels and the grid.
- `curveType` (default `monotone`), `strokeWidth`, `withDots`, `connectNulls` and `fillOpacity` shape the lines.
- `withXAxis`, `withYAxis` and `withTooltip` turn those parts off.
- The x axis drops labels that would overlap, keeping the first and the last.

## Differences from Mantine

- It lays itself out in HTML and a stretched SVG instead of recharts, so it needs no measuring and stays responsive;
  `xAxisProps`, `yAxisProps`, `gridProps`, `lineProps`, `tooltipProps`, `legendProps` and children are not supported.
- Not ported yet: `orientation: "vertical"`, the gradient line `type`, a right y axis, `withPointLabels`, the brush,
  reference areas and dots.
