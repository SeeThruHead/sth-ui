## Usage

`data` is a list of `{ name, value, color, children }` nodes; a node with children is sized by their sum and holds
them inside it. Tiles are laid out by recharts' squarify algorithm (`aspectRatio`, the golden ratio by default), take
their `color` or their parent's, or a blue shade by position, and show their name when they are big enough. Hovering a
tile shows its value.

## Colours, borders and tooltip

- `autoContrast` picks black or white text for each tile; `textColor` sets one colour for all.
- `strokeColor` and `strokeWidth` (1) draw the borders between tiles.
- `height` (300) sizes the chart, and `valueFormatter` formats the tooltip value; `withTooltip={false}` turns it off.

## Differences from Mantine

- `dataKey`, `treemapProps`, `tooltipProps` and `children` are recharts props and are not supported.
