## Usage

BulletChart draws `ranges` (each `{ value, color }`) as bands across a track scaled to the largest range, a bar for
`value` and a line for `target`, with their values marked above and below. `label` names it on the left, and
`barColor` and `targetColor` colour the bar and the target.

<Example height={90} story="charts-bulletchart--usage" caption="Revenue of 270 against a target of 250" />

## Orientation, sizes and tooltip

- `orientation: "vertical"` stands it upright.
- `size` (32) and `barSize` (16) set the track's and the bar's thickness.
- `targetRatio` (0.7) and `targetSize` (2) set the target line's length and width.
- `valueFormatter` formats every value.
- `withTooltip` shows "Value / Target" (or `getTooltipLabel({ value, target })`) above the pointer.

## Differences from Mantine

- The tooltip follows the pointer rather than sitting above the track, and `getTooltipLabel` returns a string.
