## Usage

`data` has `nodes` (`{ name, color }`) and `links` (`{ source, target, value }`, by node index). Nodes are laid out
in columns by recharts' Sankey algorithm (the same positions recharts computes): sources on the left, nodes with no
outgoing links on the right, each as tall as the larger of its incoming and outgoing totals. Labels show the name and
value beside each node, on two lines when the node is tall enough. Hovering a node or a link shows its value.

## Colours and sizes

- A node's `color` colours it and its outgoing links; other nodes take `nodeColor`, or cycle through `colors` (blue,
  cyan, teal and on by default), and their links take `linkColor` (gray) at `linkOpacity` (0.4).
- `height` (300), `nodeWidth` (10), `nodePadding` (10), `linkCurvature` (0.5) and `iterations` (32) shape the layout.
- `textColor` colours the labels, `valueFormatter` formats values, and `withTooltip={false}` turns hovering off.

## Differences from Mantine

- `sankeyProps`, `tooltipProps` and `children` are recharts props and are not supported.
