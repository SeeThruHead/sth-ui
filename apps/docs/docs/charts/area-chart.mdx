## Usage

AreaChart takes the same data, axes, grid, legend and tooltip props as [LineChart](/charts/line-chart), and fills
the area under each series with a gradient (`fillOpacity`, 0.2, at the top fading out).

<Configurator story="charts-areachart--configurator" />

## Stacked and percent

`type: "stacked"` stacks the series on one another, and `type: "percent"` stacks them to 100% of each day, with the
y axis in percent. Stacked types fill flat unless `withGradient`. `connectNulls: false` breaks an area at a missing
value, and negative values fill up to zero.

## Differences from Mantine

- It draws its own SVG rather than recharts; `areaProps`, `areaChartProps` and children are not supported.
- Not ported yet: the `split` and `stream` types, `orientation: "vertical"`, a right y axis, point labels, the brush,
  reference areas and dots.
