## Usage

`Textarea` is a multiline [TextInput](/inputs/text-input): every Input prop (variant, size, radius, sections,
loading, error, success, disabled) and every wrapper prop (label, description, error, required, withAsterisk,
inputWrapperOrder) in one component, rendering a `textarea`. Give it an `id`; it links the label, description and
error to the field.

<Configurator story="inputs-textarea--configurator" />

## Loading state

Set `loading` to show a loader in the right section, or on the left with `loadingPosition: "left"`.

<Example story="inputs-textarea--loading" caption="Loading" />

## Controlled

Keep the value in your model and update it from `onInput` (each keystroke) or `onChange` (on commit), as the
bottom section example below does.

## Uncontrolled

Without `value`, the textarea keeps its own text; give it a `name` and it is sent with its form.

## Bottom section

`bottomSection` shows content inside the textarea under the text, such as a character count; the textarea makes
room for it at the bottom.

<Example story="inputs-textarea--bottom-section" caption="A character counter that follows the model" />

## Autosize

`autosize` grows the textarea with its content, from `minRows` up to `maxRows` (no limit without it). It uses the
browser's own `field-sizing: content`, so nothing is measured in JavaScript.

<Example height={360} story="inputs-textarea--autosize" caption="4 rows and growing; 2 rows growing to 4" />

## Enable resize

`resize` (`none` by default, `vertical`, `horizontal` or `both`) lets the person drag the textarea's size.

## Differences from Mantine

- Autosize is CSS (`field-sizing: content`) instead of a measuring component; in browsers without it the textarea
  keeps its `minRows` height and scrolls.
