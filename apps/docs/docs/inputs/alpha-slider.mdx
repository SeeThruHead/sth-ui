## Usage

AlphaSlider picks an alpha value from 0 to 1 (rounded to two decimals) over a checkerboard that fades into
`color`. Keep `AlphaSlider.Model` (from `AlphaSlider.init({ id })`) and the value in your model, pass its messages
through `AlphaSlider.update(model, message, value)` and lift `AlphaSlider.subscriptions` so dragging follows the
pointer. The `Changed` out message carries the value while it moves and `ChangeEnded` when the drag or key press
ends; `ScrubStarted` and `ScrubEnded` mark the drag.

## Sizes and focus

`size` (`xs` to `xl`, `md` by default) sets the thumb, and `focusable: false` takes the slider out of the tab order.
Arrow Left and Arrow Right move it by five percent.

## Styles API

Its parts are `slider`, `sliderOverlay` (the checkerboard, the colour and the inset shadow) and `thumb`, and the
slider carries `data-alpha`.

## Differences from Mantine

- State lives in your model, and dragging needs the subscription lifted into yours.
