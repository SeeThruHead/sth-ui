## Usage

Stepper shows a sequence of steps and the content of the active one. `active` is the index of the current step (from
your model), `steps` lists the steps with their `label`, `description` and content (`children`), and `completed` is
shown once `active` is past the last step. `onStepClick` makes steps clickable.

## Allow step select

A step's `allowStepSelect` decides whether it can be clicked, whatever its state. Here a step can be selected once
it has been visited and when it is not the current one:

<Example height={300} story="navigation-stepper--allow-step-select" caption="Visited steps only" />

## Disable next steps selection

By default any step can be clicked; `allowNextStepsSelect: false` allows only the completed ones (and steps with
`allowStepSelect`). Steps that cannot be clicked are left out of the tab order.

<Example height={300} story="navigation-stepper--allow-next-steps" caption="Completed steps only" />

## Color, radius and size

`color` (the primary colour by default), `radius` (round by default) and `size` (`xs` to `xl`, `md` by default) set
the icons and text; `iconSize` sets the icon alone, and `contentPadding` the space above the content.

<Example height={140} story="navigation-stepper--color-size" caption="Teal, md radius, small" />

## With custom icons

`icon`, `progressIcon` and `completedIcon`, on the Stepper or on a step, replace the step number, the icon of the
current step and the check mark. Each can be a function of the step index, as can `label` and `description`.

## keepMounted and wrap

`keepMounted` keeps every step's content in the page and hides the inactive ones. Horizontal steps wrap onto more
lines when there is no room; `wrap: false` keeps them on one line. `autoContrast` picks a readable colour for the
icons of completed steps.

## Styles API

Its parts are `root`, `steps`, `separator`, `content`, `step`, `stepWrapper`, `stepIcon`, `stepIconContent`,
`stepCompletedIcon`, `stepBody`, `stepLabel`, `stepDescription`, `stepLoader` and `verticalSeparator`. Steps carry
`data-progress`, `data-completed`, `data-icon-position`, `data-label-position` and `data-allow-click`; separators carry
`data-active`.

## Accessibility

Each step is a button, named by its label and description or by `ariaLabel`. Tab and Shift Tab move between clickable
steps and Space or Enter selects one; steps that cannot be clicked are skipped.

## Differences from Mantine

- `Stepper.Step` and `Stepper.Completed` are the `steps` and `completed` props, and the active step lives in your
  model.
