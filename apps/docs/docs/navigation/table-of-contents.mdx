## Usage

TableOfContents lists the headings of the page and marks the one closest to the top as it scrolls. Keep
`TableOfContents.Model` (from `TableOfContents.init({ id })`) in your model and pass its messages through
`TableOfContents.update(model, message, scrollSpyOptions)`. `variant` (`filled`, `light` or `none`), `color`, `size`
and `radius` style the active control.

<Configurator story="navigation-tableofcontents--configurator" />

## Scroll spy options

`scrollSpyOptions` takes the `selector` of the headings (every `h1` to `h6` by default), the `scrollHost` to watch
(a selector; the window by default) and an `offset` from the top. The depth of a heading is the number in its tag
and its text is the control's label.

## Pass props to controls

`getControlProps` receives each heading (`data`), its `index` and whether it is `active`, and returns the control's
`children`, an `href` (which makes the control a link) or an `onClick` message. Send
`TableOfContents.Message.ScrolledTo({ index })` to scroll a heading into view, as these demos do.

## Initial data

The headings are read when the component mounts. `initialData` (headings with `id`, `value` and `depth`) is shown
until then.

## Depth offset

Controls are indented by their depth minus `minDepthToOffset` (1 by default) times `depthOffset` (`0.8em` by default):

## Styles API

Its parts are `root` and `control`. Controls carry `data-active` and `data-variant`.

## Differences from Mantine

- `getDepth` and `getValue` are not options: the depth comes from the tag and the value from the text.
- `scrollHost` is a selector, and a control scrolls by sending `ScrolledTo` instead of calling `getNode()`.
- `reinitializeRef` is the `Reinitialized` message.
