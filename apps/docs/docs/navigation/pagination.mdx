## Usage

Pagination shows the pages around the active one, with previous and next controls. Keep the active page in your
model: pass it as `value`, and `onChange` gives the page to move to (always between the first and last page).

## Siblings

`siblings` is how many pages show on each side of the active one (1 by default).

<Example height={330} story="navigation-pagination--siblings" caption="1, 2 and 3 siblings" />

## Boundaries

`boundaries` is how many pages show at each end (1 by default).

<Example height={330} story="navigation-pagination--boundaries" caption="1, 2 and 3 boundaries" />

## Responsive layout

With `layout="responsive"`, the pages give way to a "Page 1 of 20" label when the pagination is 400px wide or
narrower. `formatLabel` changes the label's text.

<Example height={140} story="navigation-pagination--responsive" caption="640px wide, and 300px wide" />

## Hide pages controls

`withPages={false}` leaves only the previous and next controls, for showing your own summary beside them.

<Example height={100} story="navigation-pagination--with-pages" caption="Rows shown, out of 145" />

## Controls as links

`getItemProps` and `getControlProps` give each page and control an `href` (and optionally a `label` or `children`),
which renders it as a link.

<Example height={100} story="navigation-pagination--links" caption="Each control links to #page-N" />

## Change icons

`nextIcon`, `previousIcon`, `firstIcon`, `lastIcon` and `dotsIcon` replace the default icons; `withEdges` adds the
first and last page controls.

<Example height={100} story="navigation-pagination--icons" caption="Arrow icons" />

## Controls size

`size` is `xs` to `xl`, or `input-xs` to `input-xl` to match the height of an input of that size.

<Example height={150} story="navigation-pagination--size" caption="sm, and input-sm beside a button and an input" />

## Start value

`startValue` is the first page; `total` stays the last page.

## Accessibility

The active page has `aria-current="page"`. The first, previous, next and last controls are labelled "First page",
"Previous page", "Next page" and "Last page"; `getControlProps` can give them other labels.

## Differences from Mantine

- Pagination is always controlled (`value` and `onChange`); there is no `defaultValue`.
- The compound parts (`Pagination.Root`, `Pagination.Items`, ...) are not ported: the props above cover the same
  layouts.
- The edge controls have accessible names by default.
