## Usage

Highlight marks substrings of its text with `<mark>` (the Mark component). Pass the text as `children` and the
substring or substrings to mark as `highlight`. Matching is case-insensitive and accent-insensitive by default, and
every occurrence is marked.

<Configurator story="typography-highlight--configurator" />

## Matching behaviour

- Case-insensitive: "hello" matches "Hello" and "HELLO" (`caseInsensitive`, on by default).
- Accent-insensitive: "cafe" matches "café" and "CAFÉ" (`accentInsensitive`, on by default), and the marked text
  keeps its accents.
- Every occurrence is marked.
- Regular expression characters such as `[`, `(` and `+` are matched literally.
- Leading and trailing whitespace in a term is ignored, and empty terms are skipped.

## Whole word matching

`wholeWord` marks only complete words, so "the" does not match "theme" or "there".

## Change highlight styles

`highlightStyles` takes StyleX styles applied to every mark.

## Text props

Highlight is built on Text and takes its props except `c` for the highlight: `color` sets the mark's background.
`component` changes the element, here a link:

<Example height={100} story="typography-highlight--text-props" caption="A highlighted link" />

## Styles API

It has Text's `root` part; marks are Mark components and carry `data-highlight` with the matched text.

## Differences from Mantine

- `highlightStyles` takes StyleX styles instead of a style object or a theme function.
