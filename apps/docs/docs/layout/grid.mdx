## Offset, auto and content

`offset` leaves that many columns empty before a column. `span="auto"` takes the space left in the row;
`span="content"` is as wide as its content.

<Example story="layout-grid--offset-auto-and-content" caption="Offset, content, auto and span" />

## Grow, columns, justify and align

`grow` makes the last row fill the width. `columns` changes the count (24 here). `justify`, `align` and
`overflow` set the row's alignment and overflow.

<Example story="layout-grid--grow-and-columns" caption="Grow, 24 columns, a centred column" />

## By breakpoint

`span`, `offset`, `order`, `align` and `gutter` take an object by breakpoint. Here the columns stack, in
reverse order, on phones and sit side by side from `md`.

<Example story="layout-grid--responsive-on-desktop" caption="Side by side from md (try Phone)" />
