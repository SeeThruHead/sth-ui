## Ratio

`ratio` is width divided by height (`16 / 9`, `4 / 3`); the default is 1, a square.

<Example story="layout-aspectratio--square-by-default" caption="Square, and 1 by 2" />

## Images, video and embeds

Every direct child gets the ratio and the full width, like Mantine's `& > *` rule, and an image or video child also
gets `object-fit: cover`, so a bare `img` fills the box. A child positioned absolutely, such as an Overlay, sits on
top without taking room.
