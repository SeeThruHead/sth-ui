## Usage

Card is a [Paper](/miscellaneous/paper) with a padding of its own and sections that reach its edges. If you
don't need sections, use Paper.

`orientation="horizontal"` lays the card out in a row; sections then reach its top and bottom edges.

## Card sections

`cardSection` removes the card's padding from a section:

- The first child of the card gets negative top and side margins, so it reaches the top edge.
- The last child gets negative bottom and side margins.
- One in the middle only gets negative side margins.

Mantine finds the first and last section with CSS; StyleX styles an element only by its own classes, so here
the card places each `cardSection` among its children itself.

```ts
ui.Card(h, {
  padding: "xl",
  children: [
    cardSection({ children: ["First section"] }),
    ui.Text(h, { children: ["Some other content"] }),
    cardSection({ children: ["Middle section"] }),
    cardSection({ children: ["Last section"] })
  ]
})
```

## withBorder and inheritPadding

`withBorder` draws borders between a section and its neighbours (not along the card's own edge), and
`inheritPadding` gives the section the card's side padding. A section takes style props (`py`, `mt`...) and its
own `styles`.
