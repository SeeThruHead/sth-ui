## Initials

To show initials instead of the default placeholder, set `name` to the person's name, such as
`name: "John Doe"`. With a name set, `color: "initials"` picks a colour from it, the same one Mantine would:

<Example story="data-display-avatar--initials" caption="Seven names, each with its own colour" />

## Allowed initials colors

By default every theme colour except gray and dark can be picked for initials. `allowedInitialsColors` limits
the choice:

<Example story="data-display-avatar--allowed-colors" caption="Only blue and red" />

## Placeholder

When the image can't be loaded or there is no `src`, Avatar shows a placeholder: its `children`, the initials of
`name`, or an icon. `alt` becomes the placeholder's `title`.

<Example story="data-display-avatar--placeholders" caption="Default, indigo, initials and a star" />

## Variants

`variant` is `light` (the default), `filled`, `outline`, `transparent`, `white`, `default` or `gradient`;
`color` defaults to gray.

<Example story="data-display-avatar--variants" caption="Every variant in blue" />

<Example story="data-display-avatar--sizes" caption="xs to xl, 100 pixels and 3rem with radius md" />

## AvatarGroup

`AvatarGroup` stacks avatars, overlapping each by `spacing` (default `sm`). Give it the avatars' props as its
children:

<Example story="data-display-avatar--group" caption="Three pictures and +5" />

## Polymorphic component

`component` renders the avatar as another element, such as a link to the person's profile.
