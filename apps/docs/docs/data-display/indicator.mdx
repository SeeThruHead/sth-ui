## Usage

Indicator puts a small dot or label at a corner of its children: `top-end` by default, any of `top`, `middle` or
`bottom` with `start`, `center` or `end`. `size` is 10px by default, `color` the primary color.

<Configurator story="datadisplay-indicator--configurator" />

## Inline

`inline` sizes the indicator's box to its children, for children that are inline, such as an avatar.

<Example height={110} story="datadisplay-indicator--inline" caption="A label on an inline avatar" />

## Offset

`offset` moves the indicator in from the corner (a number, or `{ x, y }`); `withBorder` rings it in the page
background.

<Example height={110} story="datadisplay-indicator--offset" caption="7px in from the bottom right, with a border" />

## Disabled

`disabled` hides the indicator.

<Example height={160} story="datadisplay-indicator--disabled" caption="Hidden until toggled" />

## Max value

A number `label` above `maxValue` shows as `maxValue+`.

<Example height={110} story="datadisplay-indicator--max-value" caption="50, 100 and 1000" />

## Show zero

`showZero` (on by default) decides whether a `0` label shows.

<Example height={110} story="datadisplay-indicator--show-zero" caption="A zero shown and hidden" />

## Auto contrast

`autoContrast` picks black or white text to suit the color.
